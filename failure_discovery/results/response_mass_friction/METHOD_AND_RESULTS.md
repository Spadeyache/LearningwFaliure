# Intervention-response test

90 recorded attempts across 9 mass/friction settings and 10 seeds. 51 failed cases form 7 observed response profiles.

No failure names were supplied: each group contains failed cases with the same recorded target-setting outcomes. Changing mass keeps friction fixed; changing friction keeps mass fixed. Joint settings are checked separately. No-change targets are marked as controls in the evidence CSV. No missing trial is assumed to have failed.

5 failed cases had no successful single-variable target among these levels, but had a successful tested joint change. This is a bounded experimental result, not a universal claim that both parameters must change.

These are physical interventions on the simulator, not grip-command changes or updates to an estimated policy input. All selected trials come from the custom-policy dataset, not the stock policy.

Different response profiles do not prove distinct physical mechanisms; equal profiles need not mean equal causes. Repeatability across these ten seeds is descriptive. The next test is finer intervention levels and additional held-out starts.
