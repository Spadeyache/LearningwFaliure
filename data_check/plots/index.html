B1 · Single-variable success curves

Nominal plus isolated shifts only; 10 matched seeds per point. Bars: 95% Wilson intervals (descriptive; pairing used in the experiment).

B2 · Pair interactions with other variables held nominal

Missing cells stay missing. Mixed episodes with additional changed factors are excluded; no averaging over geometry/COM confounds.

B3 · Do combined changes cancel or compound?

Same 10 seeds per point. Compare combination to each isolated shift; curves are descriptive and do not prove a unique failure cause.

B4 · Rule labels by changed variable

Single-variable shifts only; successful and failed attempts included. "Cause" here means experimental intervention, not identified mechanism.

C1 · Raw measurement traces colored by final rule label

Thin lines: all episodes. Bold lines: outcome-group mean. These groups mix physical conditions; this is an exploratory view.

C2 · Condition gap overview; individual trace pages linked below

Derived shifted − same-seed nominal; common nominal-lift clock. Relative/world orientation gaps use rotation vectors, not quaternion subtraction.

C3 · Mass failures versus object-friction failures

Isolated interventions and failed episodes only. Mean ± 1 SD; conditioning on failure and nominal lifting changes the represented population.

C4 · Signature scaling at the magnitudes actually collected

Requested ×0.5/×2/×4 series is unavailable. Mass: ×0.4/×5; object friction: ×0.25/×2.5. Other actual levels shown; mean ± 1 SD.

C5 · Channels × isolated interventions

Mean ± 1 SD; both magnitudes pooled, so opposite signed effects can cancel. Use individual-condition pages to inspect each level.

C6 · Exploratory time of divergence

First 3 consecutive samples beyond preset effect thresholds, ≥3 valid runs. Not a statistical alarm; thresholds saved in scores.json. — means not detected.

D1 · Separability using one measurement channel at a time

Predicts which variable was shifted, not why an individual attempt failed. All isolated shifts included; scale fitted on training folds only.

D2 · Measurement consistency versus separation

Descriptive full-sample standardization for this plot only. High across-variable separation and low within-variable spread are desirable; scores use held-out seeds.

D3 · How early is the intervention identifiable?

Only samples at/before each cutoff are used. Nominal-lift timing is privileged offline alignment; 9 seeds eligible. This is not a deployment latency estimate.

D4 · Correlation between episode-mean gap channels

One point per isolated-shift episode; timesteps are not treated as independent samples. Blank cells indicate constant channels. Shared seeds still induce dependence.

E1 · PCA gap atlas: isolated interventions

Standardized reset-aligned gaps; exploratory fit on singles only. ▼ lower/negative level; ▲ higher/positive level; P sphere. Actual magnitude mapping is in conditions.csv.

E2 · Exactly the same atlas colored by rule label

A rule label describes an outcome, while the experimental label describes a changed variable. PCA is descriptive and does not prove cluster validity.

E3 · Mixed conditions projected onto the single-variable atlas

Scaler and PCA fitted only on singles; combinations are transformed without refitting. Being between groups is a visual pattern, not evidence of additive causes.

F1 · Selected-channel classification: 28.7% accuracy

Five best channels ranked using only each training fold; 5-fold grouped by starting seed. Classes are interventions, not proven failure mechanisms.

F2 · Rule label versus changed variable

Counts of isolated-shift episodes. A single outcome such as drop can follow several different interventions; success is included.

F3 · Accuracy versus measurement count

Channel ranking is recomputed inside each training fold. This curve is exploratory; choosing its best count would require an additional independent test set.

F4 · Held-out classification by actual shift magnitude

Same out-of-fold predictions as F1; 10 starts per condition. Bars: 95% Wilson intervals. No missing magnitudes are invented.

F5 · Singles → combinations: exact match 1.1%, micro-F1 0.42

8 binary detectors; training uses singles + nominal only. Test combinations have held-out seeds. Threshold 0.5 fixed in advance; combinations are multi-label.

G1 · Commanded motor limit versus contact force; command-ceiling use

Contact force can exceed a motor cap during impacts. Actual actuator effort/torque saturation is not logged, so true motor-limit hits cannot be established.

G2 · Gripper opening and relative motion around failure

Two populations kept separate: detected slip/drop onset versus unknown physical onset. Relative velocity is in a moving frame; sag versus slip is not directly labeled.

H1 · Task demand and achieved lift versus physical properties

All episodes; mixed conditions are confounded. Center lift and bottom clearance differ when geometry rotates. These scatter plots are descriptive, not isolated causal effects.

H2 · Transfer of mass/friction signatures across geometric changes

Train only on isolated mass/friction controls; test held-out-seed mixed episodes with exactly one dynamic shift plus geometry/COM. Other mixed factors may still confound; strata overlap.
