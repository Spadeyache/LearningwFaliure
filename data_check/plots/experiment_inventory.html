All collected experiment patterns

60 unique settings × 10 starting seeds = 600 attempts. These are one dataset with several experiment designs.

Breakdown: 1 nominal reference + 15 isolated shifts + 12 designed paired shifts + 32 mixed settings. The nominal reference is shared, not an extra ten trials for every comparison.

Both pair directions were collected for mass/object-friction, mass/size and mass/lift-height: two same-direction settings and two opposite-direction settings per pair. These use ordered low/high levels; they are not a continuous linear sweep. All nine mass/friction grid cells were covered, including the nominal and single-variable settings.

Nominal: 100 g, object friction 0.4, 40 mm cube, COM X=0 mm, lift 20 cm, finger friction 0.2, table friction 0.3. The policy estimate is 40 g throughout.

Every row uses the same ten seeds 2000–2009, with seeded initial positions/orientations. Shape or size changes can change the support height. Each plot link opens its isolated situation, including nominal reference trials.
